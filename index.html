Our story

Born in Mogadishu.
Built on coffee.

Salool Café was founded with one goal — bring world-class specialty coffee to Mogadishu. Today, three branches and thousands of loyal cups later, we’re just getting started.

Every drink is made with intention: the right beans, the right milk, the right amount of care. Our people, our craft, and the little details are what make your cup unmistakably Salool.
