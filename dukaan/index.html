How it works

Three steps. No queue.

Order from your phone, pay the way you already pay, and we’ll have it ready.

  1. 01

    Build your order

    Pick from the menu and add it to your cart. Minimum order is $5.

  2. 02

    Choose pickup or delivery

    Delivery is $1.50, free over $15. Pickup is always free.

  3. 03

    Pay and track

    Send the total by EVC Plus, or pay cash. You’ll get an order number and a WhatsApp receipt.
