How it works

Three steps, and it's at their door.

  1. 01

    Choose and schedule

    Pick a gift, add your message, and choose the day and window that suits them — birthdays, Eid mornings, a surprise Tuesday.

  2. 02

    We confirm by phone

    Our team calls the recipient to agree on a time, so nobody misses the delivery and the gift arrives when they're actually home.

  3. 03

    Delivered, with proof

    The courier hands it over, photographs it, and messages you on WhatsApp. You see their face even from six thousand miles away.
